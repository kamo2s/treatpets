How it works

From a trade to a shelter's doorstep

Treat only pays. It never holds an item, never ships anything and never publishes a shelter's address. These services do the rest, and each one leaves a record you can open.

pump.fun

Someone trades $TREAT

Every buy and sell pays a small creator fee. All of it goes to the treasury.

Record: each fee claim on Solscan
Jupiter · USDC

The fees become a wish fund

The treasury swaps them to USDC. 90% goes to the fund, the rest to the team that runs it.

Record: each swap on Solscan
Chewy

A shelter's wishes are picked

Shelters keep public wish lists. We only read them and pick the next shelter: never helped first, most needed first.

Record: a dated copy on the Wayback Machine
Amazon match

The same items, found on Amazon

Each wish is matched to the same product: same brand, same size, close price.

Record: each wish next to its Amazon listing
SP3ND

Paid in USDC, bought by SP3ND

SP3ND prices the order with tax and shipping, the treasury pays it in one USDC transaction, and SP3ND buys it on Amazon.

Record: the payment on Solscan, memo names the order
Delivery

Delivered to the shelter

Amazon ships the box to the shelter's public address, like any gift from a wish list. The shelter signs up for nothing.

Record: order number and carrier tracking

The rules the fund follows

No one picks favourites. The same three rules decide every order.

Never helped first

Shelters that haven't received a box yet go to the front of the line. Then the ones helped longest ago.

Most needed first

Inside a list, items the shelter marked "Most needed" are bought before everything else.

Exact match or skip

If Amazon doesn't sell the same brand and size at a close price, the wish is skipped. Nothing is swapped for something else.
